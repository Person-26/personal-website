How this site is drawn

Everything you see here is simulated light. The screen is the middle of a waveguide, a slab of space between two ends just past the left and right edges of the window. Nothing on the screen is drawn directly: the only input is the light each end sends in. Where that light crosses in the middle, it interferes into the page.

The light is three frequencies, blue, green and red, which is enough for the eye, as in a laser projector. What each end must send is worked out from the page, but light can only carry shapes larger than about half a wavelength, so you'll see faint rings and coloured fringes. When a page loads or you scroll, the light has to travel in from the edges, so the page forms from the sides inward.
